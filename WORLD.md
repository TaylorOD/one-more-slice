# snowcrust world notes

The restaurant is C0saN0stra Pizza. Snowcrust is the project.

## what belongs here

The opening chapters give the franchise automated dispatch chutes, procedure manuals, smart pizza boxes that carry addresses, and a thirty minute guarantee. The kitchen now has a chute, scanner terminal and Pizza University binder. The parcel has an address panel.

RadiKS kouriers wear orange and blue padded uniforms and use smartwheels and electromagnetic harpoons. Those details guide the rider, board and pickup animation.

U-Stor-It in Inglewood is storage space used as housing. Corrugated doors sit behind the traffic lanes. Greater Hong Kong is a franchulate, represented by a separate entry checkpoint. Neither blocks the rider's route.

The Black Sun exists in the Metaverse. A caller works on avatars there but receives food at a physical motel. It is not a street building.

## adaptation choices

The callers, their dialogue, jobs and orders are original. The map compresses separate locations into one readable delivery scene. The floating prep station is a play interface.

A ticket arrives 28 minutes into the guarantee. Two minutes remain for play. This is a game convention, not a timing claim from the novel. The score still measures toppings and the bite penalty separately from delivery time.
